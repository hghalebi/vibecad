## Next Step: Fix the Horizontal Flange Holes

The horizontal flange holes must drill **vertically (Z-axis)** through the bottom flange (which lies in the Z=0 plane, thickness=3mm).

**Replace `h_hole1` and `h_hole2`** with cylinders oriented along **Z-axis**, positioned at:
- X positions: `hole_pos1=12.5` and `hole_pos2=37.5` (across the 50mm extrusion width)  
- Y position: `flange_length - hole_inset = 50 - 9 = 41mm` from origin (inset from free edge)
- Z position: centered through the 3mm thickness → use `Pos(x, 41, 0)` with cylinder along Z

```python
# Horizontal flange holes - drill through Z (vertical direction)
h_hole1 = Pos(hole_pos1, flange_length - hole_inset, 0) * \
    Cylinder(hole_dia/2, thickness * 4,
             align=(Align.CENTER, Align.CENTER, Align.CENTER))

h_hole2 = Pos(hole_pos2, flange_length - hole_inset, 0) * \
    Cylinder(hole_dia/2, thickness * 4,
             align=(Align.CENTER, Align.CENTER, Align.CENTER))

# Vertical flange holes - drill through X direction  
v_hole1 = Pos(0, hole_pos1, hole_inset) * Rot(0, 90, 0) * \
    Cylinder(hole_dia/2, thickness * 4,
             align=(Align.CENTER, Align.CENTER, Align.CENTER))

v_hole2 = Pos(0, hole_pos2, hole_inset) * Rot(0, 90, 0) * \
    Cylinder(hole_dia/2, thickness * 4,
             align=(Align.CENTER, Align.CENTER, Align.CENTER))
```

This will give **2 holes per flange = 4 total**, all correctly oriented and inset 9mm from the free edges — industry standard for this bracket type.
