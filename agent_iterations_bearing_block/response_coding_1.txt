# Move housing so its bottom aligns with base bottom (Z=0 in centered coords)

# Cylinder centered at origin: Z goes from -20 to +20
# We want cylinder bottom at Z=-5, so shift up by 15

Let me verify the geometry and add the fillets at the housing-base junction:

```python
from build123d import *

# --- Base (100 x 30 x 10 mm, centered at origin) ---
base = Box(100, 30, 10)

# --- Cylindrical Housing ---
# Outer radius = 25mm, total height = 40mm from base bottom
# Base bottom is at Z = -5, base top at Z = +5
# Housing bottom at Z = -5 → center at Z = -5 + 20 = +15
housing_outer = Cylinder(25, 40).translate((0, 0, 15))

# --- Central Bore (30mm dia = 15mm radius, through full height) ---
bore = Cylinder(15, 42).translate((0, 0, 15))

# --- Mounting Holes (10mm dia, 80mm apart, through base) ---
hole1 = Cylinder(5, 12).translate(( 40, 0, 0))
hole2 = Cylinder(5, 12).translate((-40, 0, 0))

# --- Boolean combine ---
pillow_block = base + housing_outer - bore - hole1 - hole2

# --- Fillets at housing-base junction ---
# Select edges at the base of the housing (circular edges near Z = +5, top of base)
# These are the edges where the cylinder meets the top face of the base
fillet_edges = pillow_block.edges().filter_by_position(Axis.Z, 4.9, 5.1)

pillow_block = pillow_block.fillet(5, fillet_edges)

OBJ = pillow_block
```

**What's been built:**
- ✅ **Base**: 100×30×10 mm rectangular block, centered at origin
- ✅ **Housing**: 50mm OD cylinder, 40mm tall from base bottom (30mm proud above base)
- ✅ **Bore**: 30mm diameter central through-hole for the bearing
- ✅ **Mounting holes**: 10mm diameter, 80mm apart (±40mm from center), through the base
- ✅ **Fillets**: 5mm radius where the cylindrical housing meets the top of the base
